# Zi Wei Dou Shu: Horoscope periods（紫微斗數運限）   {#紫微斗数运限}

## Types of time cycles {#运限的分类}

If the [natal chart（本命盤）](./astrolabe.md) is a map fixed at birth, time cycles（運限） describe your movement through different parts of that map at different times. The natal chart describes a person’s basic traits, habitual patterns, and possibilities; time cycles indicate when those traits are more likely to be activated.

Time cycles（運限） in Zi Wei Dou Shu（紫微斗數） can be divided down to the `minute-level cycle`（流分） and `second-level cycle`（流秒） levels. However, finer detail brings more chance factors and a greater risk of misleading interpretations. For everyday chart reading, focus on the following five categories:

- `decadal`（大限）: A cycle covering ten years, forming the main framework for assessing time cycles（運限）. It begins in the soul palace（命宮） and progresses forward for yang-year men and yin-year women, or backward for yin-year men and yang-year women. The [Five Elements Class（五行局）](./basis.md#五行局) determines the starting age of the first cycle.
- `age`（小限）: Advances one palace per year and uses nominal age（虛歲）. Its starting point depends on the trine group（三合局） of the birth year’s Earthly Branch（地支）. It moves forward for men and backward for women. See [Rules for placing the annual age cycle（起小限訣）](./setup.md#_5-起小限诀) for the procedure.
- `yearly`（流年）: A chart showing the themes of a single year. Its soul palace（命宮） occupies the palace corresponding to that year’s Earthly Branch（地支）, advancing one palace each year. The year also has its own `transit stars`（流曜） and `yearly four transformations`（流年四化）.
- `monthly`（流月）: Breaks the yearly cycle’s themes down by month. It helps identify which months favor progress on a matter that is already showing signs of unfolding. It should not be used alone to predict major events without considering the decadal（大限） and yearly（流年） cycles.
- `daily`（流日） and `hourly`（流時）: Narrow the time frame to a day and a traditional two-hour period, respectively. They are generally used to choose timing or review specific events. Beginners can easily become overwhelmed by this level of detail, so first become comfortable with decadal（大限） and yearly（流年） cycles.

Decadal（大限）, annual age（小限）, and yearly（流年） cycles are not interchangeable charts. The decadal cycle gives the broader environment of ten years; the yearly cycle highlights that year’s focus; and the annual age cycle adds the areas likely to affect the individual more strongly that year. For example, a decadal cycle in the career palace（官祿宮） does not mean that every event over those ten years concerns work. It does mean that work, direction, responsibilities, and social roles deserve closer attention during that period.

The Complete Book of Zi Wei Dou Shu（紫微斗數全書） summarizes the rules for starting decadal cycles（大限） directly:

> 大限由命宮起，陽男陰女順行；陰男陽女逆行，每十年過一宮限。

## What time cycles tell us {#运限的作用}

After receiving a chart, many people first ask, “When will I become wealthy?”, “When will I marry?”, or “When will I change jobs?” Answering these questions requires examining time cycles（運限）. The natal chart（本命盤） is like the ingredients, while the time cycles are like the cooking conditions: the same ingredient can produce different results depending on how it is cooked.

Time cycles（運限） do not turn the future into an unchangeable script. They help us assess which themes and obstacles are more likely at a particular stage and where to focus our attention. They serve at least three purposes:

1.   **Identify the themes of a stage.** Whichever natal palace the decadal cycle’s soul palace（大限命宮） reaches, that palace’s themes receive greater emphasis. Reaching the career palace（官祿宮） does not guarantee a promotion; reaching the spouse palace（夫妻宮） does not guarantee marriage. Rather, these placements suggest that matters such as career direction, cooperation, and close relationships will take up more of your attention during those ten years.
2.   **Assess how events may unfold over time.** First use the decadal cycle（大限） to ask whether a certain kind of opportunity exists, then the yearly cycle（流年） to ask whether it is activated that year. Use the annual age（小限） and monthly（流月） cycles to narrow the time frame. Think of the decade as providing the soil and the year as providing the rain: when both conditions align, an event is more likely to take shape.
3.   **Identify risks and resources.** When a time cycle（運限） connects with both auspicious stars（吉星） and favorable transformations（吉化）, it often indicates more resources, support, or options. Encountering malefic stars and the D transformation（煞忌） does not make trouble inevitable, but suggests paying closer attention to costs, emotions, contracts, health, or interpersonal friction. Recognizing risks and preparing in advance is one of the most practical uses of time-cycle analysis.

Classical texts repeatedly stress that decadal-cycle（大限） analysis must consider more than one layer:

> 須詳大限獨守吉凶何如，小限獨守吉凶何如，太歲獨守吉凶何如。如歲限俱凶則凶。
>
> 又看大限與小限相逢吉凶何如，大限逢太歲吉凶何如，小限逢太歲吉凶何如，禍福所定。

The most useful lesson from this passage today is its method, rather than simply labeling things “good” or “bad.” Do not frighten yourself over a single yearly D transformation（流年化忌）, or sit back waiting for good things because of a single yearly A transformation（流年化祿）. An assessment is more useful when the natal（本命）, decadal（大限）, and yearly（流年） layers corroborate one another.

## How to interpret time cycles {#运限的运用}

A major pitfall in time-cycle（運限） analysis is skipping the natal chart（本命盤） and applying stock predictions directly to a particular year. That is like deciding whether to go out from the calendar alone, without checking the map or the weather; it is unlikely to be accurate. A more reliable order is:

1.   **Start with the natal chart（本命盤）.** Understand the basic characteristics of the soul palace（命宮）, body palace（身宮）, and twelve palaces（十二宮）. Pay particular attention to the areas of strength and the recurring challenges they represent. Without this foundation, time-cycle（運限） analysis has no context.
2.   **Then examine the decadal soul palace（大限命宮） and its trine and opposite palaces（三方四正）.** First identify the natal palace it occupies and examine that palace’s major stars（主星）, auxiliary stars（輔星）, malefic stars and D transformation（煞忌）, and natal four transformations（本命四化）. Then examine the decadal soul palace’s trine and opposite palaces. Together they describe the decade’s main themes; do not draw a conclusion from one palace alone.
3.   **Add the yearly layer（流年）.** To assess that year’s events, examine where the yearly soul palace（流年命宮） falls within the decadal chart, where the yearly four transformations（流年四化） land, and whether transit stars（流曜） activate important palaces in the natal or decadal chart. A favorable decade with an ordinary year often means steady accumulation. A demanding decade with favorable yearly transformations may offer a breakthrough amid pressure.
4.   **Finally, narrow the time frame with annual age（小限） and monthly（流月） cycles.** Once you understand the likely themes of the decade and year, use these cycles to examine more specific time windows. The annual age cycle has no independent transit stars（流曜）. In the library, its four transformations（四化） are calculated from the stem of its palace（宮干）, but that does not make it a separate yearly chart.

For a practical example, suppose the decadal soul palace（大限命宮） falls in the natal career palace（官祿宮）, and the yearly A（化祿） and B（化權） transformations connect with the career（官祿） or surface（遷移） palace. That year may favor actively seeking a position, project, client, or broader opportunities. If malefic stars and the D transformation（煞忌） are also involved, greater opportunity may come with greater pressure. The useful advice is not “you will definitely be promoted” or “you will definitely have bad luck,” but to strengthen your skills beforehand, clarify authority and responsibilities, and pace yourself so that opportunities do not become exhausting.

:::tip Tips
When studying time cycles（運限）, write down what is happening and look back a year or two later to compare it with your reading. Zi Wei Dou Shu（紫微斗數） is not a fill-in-the-blank exercise: a chart usually shows tendencies and circumstances. How people respond to opportunities and manage risks affects how events ultimately unfold.
:::

To repeat: time-cycle（運限） analysis should not create anxiety. For a favorable year, prepare carefully and actively pursue opportunities. For a demanding year, take more time over major decisions and leave reserves of cash and energy. Doing this is far more useful than merely memorizing prediction formulas.
