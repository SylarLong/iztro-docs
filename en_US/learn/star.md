# Zi Wei Dou Shu: Star system（紫微斗數星曜系統）   {#紫微斗数星曜系统}

## Introduction   {#前言}

The star（星曜） system in Zi Wei Dou Shu（紫微斗數） is complex because of its many combinations. You do not need to memorize them all. Personifying each star, remembering its core traits, and deriving other characteristics from them can make learning easier. There are `117` fixed stars and `21` transit stars（流曜）, excluding the ways in which the four transformations（四化星） move between palaces. Fortunately, you only need to master some stars individually and learn the others by category.

The 117 stars comprise [`14 major stars`](./major-star.md), [`14 auxiliary stars`](./minor-star.md), [`37 miscellaneous stars`](./adj-star.md), [`48 symbolic stars`（48神煞）](./dec-star.md), and the [`four transformation stars`（四化星）](./mutagen.md). Although there are only four transformations, their workings are the most complex part of the system, so they have a separate chapter. This chapter focuses on the other stars.

## Fourteen major stars {#十四主星}

The [fourteen major stars（十四主星）](./major-star.md) are also called the fourteen `principal stars`（正曜）. As `major stars`（主星）, they play a `primary` role in the chart; their task is to `set the overall character` of a palace. If a palace is a `classroom`, the major stars are its `teacher`: whether the class excels at Chinese or English depends on the teacher’s strengths. The fourteen stars form two teams led by `emperor`（紫微） and `empress`（天府）. To place all fourteen, first locate emperor（紫微）. See [Rules for locating emperor（起紫微星訣）](./setup.md#_8-起紫微星诀) if you would like to study the procedure.

The `emperor`（紫微） team has six members:

- emperor（紫微）
- advisor（天機）
- sun（太陽）
- warrior（武曲）
- fortunate（天同）
- judge（廉貞）

There are 8 members in the `empress`（天府） team. They are:

- empress（天府）
- moon（太陰）
- wolf（貪狼）
- advocator（巨門）
- minister（天相）
- sage（天梁）
- marshal（七殺）
- rebel（破軍）

## Fourteen auxiliary stars（輔星） {#十四辅星}

The [fourteen auxiliary stars（十四輔星）](./minor-star.md) act as `student class representatives`. In an `empty palace`（空宮）, the traits of any `auxiliary stars`（輔星） present become more pronounced because no major star constrains them. The fourteen auxiliary stars fall into three groups: `six auspicious stars`（六吉星）, `six malefic stars`（六煞星）, and `two supporting stars`（二助星）.

:::tip Tips

`auspicious stars`（吉星） and `malefic stars`（煞星） are traditional labels, and we should look beyond them today. Auspicious stars often suggest sociability and seem approachable; malefic stars suggest less conventional traits and may seem less easy to fit in. Yet, as the saying `人不可貌相` reminds us, appearances do not tell the whole story. The same applies to stars: a classification as `auspicious stars`（吉星） does not make a star wholly good, nor does classification as `malefic stars`（煞星） make it wholly bad.

:::

## Thirty-seven miscellaneous stars {#三十七杂曜}

Zi Wei Dou Shu（紫微斗數） has many [miscellaneous stars（雜曜）](./adj-star.md), which are easier to remember by category. Stars in the same category usually have similar roles and meanings. In our classroom analogy, `major stars`（主星） are the `teacher`, `auxiliary stars`（輔星） are the `student class representatives`, and `miscellaneous stars`（雜曜） are the `students`. Together they shape the class, or palace. Another analogy comes from games: the major stars are the `character`, the auxiliary stars the `equipment`, and miscellaneous stars the `buffs` or `debuffs`. Miscellaneous stars can be grouped into the following nine categories:

- [Social interaction](./adj-star.md#交际类)
- [Aloofness](./adj-star.md#孤傲类)
- [Arts and talents](./adj-star.md#才艺类)
- [Distinction and status](./adj-star.md#矜贵类)
- [Inner life](./adj-star.md#精神类)
- [Luck](./adj-star.md#幸运类)
- [Health](./adj-star.md#健康类)
- [Rewards](./adj-star.md#封赏类)
- [Thought and introspection](./adj-star.md#思想类)

## Forty-eight symbolic stars（神煞） {#四十八神煞}

There are two arrangements of symbolic stars（神煞）: one is fixed in the palaces at birth, and the other moves with the yearly soul palace（流年命宮）. The forty-eight symbolic stars usually do not act independently. Compared with miscellaneous stars（雜曜）, they are more like `additional attributes`, often expressed as `mental power` or `temperament`. Think of `enchantments` on game equipment or inserted `runes` that increase or decrease a particular `attribute`. Beginners can therefore leave them aside while learning to read charts.

- [Twelve growth-stage stars（長生十二神）](./dec-star.md#长生12神)
- [Twelve Doctor-cycle stars（博士十二神）](./dec-star.md#博士12神)
- [Twelve General-front stars（將前十二神）](./dec-star.md#将前12神)
- [Twelve Year-front stars（歲前十二神）](./dec-star.md#岁前12神)

::: info Tips

In the chapters on the [fourteen major stars（十四主星）](./major-star.md) and [fourteen auxiliary stars（十四輔星）](./minor-star.md), each star has a profile card containing the following information:

- [Yin Yang and five elements（五行）](./basis.md#阴阳五行)

  A star’s Yin–Yang（陰陽） and Five Elements（五行） qualities give a broad characterization, much like the personality descriptions commonly associated with blood types. The distinctions become more apparent when comparing stars with otherwise similar traits.

- Star group

  Stars are grouped into the `Central star system`（中天星系）, `Southern Dipper star system`（南斗星系）, and `Northern Dipper star system`（北斗星系）. This distinction has negligible influence on chart interpretation and is mainly useful for background knowledge or deeper study.

- Symbolic nature

  The author explains symbolic nature（化氣） through an analogy: the substance remaining after something is burned represents its essence. Here the term refers to a star’s fundamental nature. Knowing its `symbolic nature` helps you quickly remember its characteristic qualities.

- Element color

  Element colors do not affect chart interpretation. I include them for completeness because they appear in my study notes, although I no longer remember the source.

- Energy color

  The same note applies as for `Element color`.

- Career

  The occupation here belongs to the star’s symbolic role, not the chart owner’s actual job. It provides a quick impression of the star and helps explain the personality represented in the chart.

- Role

  Like `occupation`, this role is a broad summary of the star’s characteristics.

- Alias

  These nicknames were coined by later generations to reflect the stars’ characteristics. Remembering a star’s `alias` helps you become familiar with its nature more quickly.

:::

## Star brightness（星曜）   {#星曜的亮度}

The idea of star brightness（星曜亮度） in Zi Wei Dou Shu（紫微斗數） draws on how different intensities of light feel to us. The midday sun can feel hot and dazzling, while the setting sun feels gentler. This underlies the saying that a sun star（太陽） in a weak position resembles moon（太陰）, while a moon star in a strong position resembles sun.

Different Zi Wei Dou Shu（紫微斗數） schools use somewhat different brightness ratings.

Some schools use four brightness levels: `[+3]`（廟）, `[+2]`（旺）, `[0]`（利）, and `[-3]`（陷）.

Others use seven brightness levels: `[+3]`（廟）, `[+2]`（旺）, `[+1]`（得）, `[0]`（利）, `[-1]`（平）, `[-2]`（不）, and `[-3]`（陷）.

`[+3]`（廟） is the brightest level and `[-3]`（陷） the dimmest. In Zi Wei Dou Shu（紫微斗數）, brightness can be understood as a star’s urge to express its traits. Brighter stars express them more actively; dimmer stars are more reserved. For example, a bright wolf（貪狼） pursues its desires more directly, while a dim wolf is more likely to keep them private or weigh up the pursuit more carefully. A bright sun（太陽） pursues authority more actively and directly; a dim sun is gentler.

:::tip Tips
Personality differences in Zi Wei Dou Shu（紫微斗數） become clear through comparison. Warrior（武曲） is stubborn, but a warrior chart with the body palace（身宮） in the soul palace（命宮） is more stubborn still. Add the B transformation（化權） to warrior in that configuration, and the stubbornness becomes even stronger.
:::

## Transit stars（流曜）   {#流曜}

Transit stars（流曜） change with time and correspond to natal stars（本命星曜）. This system includes transit stars for decadal（大限） and yearly（流年） cycles; some schools also place them for other cycles. Their effects apply only to their current cycle. In Chinese names, decadal transit stars receive the prefix 運, while yearly transit stars receive 流. There is no decadal considery（解神）; its yearly form is called `considery(Y)`（年解）.

Schools use transit stars（流曜） differently, so this program lists them as comprehensively as possible for each school to use as needed. If any are missing, please add me on WeChat (iztroi) and let me know. I would appreciate it. The following stars have time-dependent forms:

- assistant（天魁）
- aide（天鉞）
- scholar（文昌）
- artist（文曲）
- attractive（紅鸞）
- cheerful（天喜）
- money（祿存）
- driven（擎羊）
- tangled（陀羅）
- horse（天馬）
- considery（解神）
- four transformations（四化）
